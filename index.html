Privacy Policy - Color Pop

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Developer: Wryzer Studio

Color Pop is an offline color-matching game developed by Wryzer Studio.

Information Collection

Color Pop does not require registration or personal information. The app stores game information such as best scores and preferences locally on the device. This information is not transmitted to the developer by the game's core functionality.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

The current version does not include advertisements or intentionally integrate analytics or crash-reporting services.

Data Sharing

The game's core functionality does not share personal information with third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game information can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it, subject to device backup and restoration settings. Color Pop does not provide user accounts or maintain server-side player profiles.

Children's Privacy

Color Pop is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children. The app does not knowingly request personal information from children.

Security

Color Pop is designed to minimize personal data handling. Locally stored information is subject to the security of the user's device.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change.
